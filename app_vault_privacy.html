App Privacy Policy

Calculator Vault

Privacy Policy for our secure vault and file hiding Android application.

Last Updated: September 3, 2026

Introduction

Calculator Vault ("the App") is designed to help users securely hide and protect personal photos, videos, and audio files on their Android devices. Protecting user privacy is our highest priority.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the application accesses, how it is used, and how it is protected.

Information Collection

Calculator Vault does not require account registration and does not collect personally identifiable information such as:

  • Name
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number
  • Contacts
  • Messages
  • Personal Documents

We do not upload personal photos, videos, audio files, or vault content to our servers.

These permissions are used only to access files selected by the user. The application does not access files without user interaction.

Third-Party Services

The application may use trusted third-party services to provide analytics, advertising, and billing functionality.

  • Google AdMob
  • Meta Platforms, Inc. (Meta App Events SDK)
  • Google Analytics for Firebase
  • Google Play Billing
  • Google Play Services

Certain third-party services used by the application, such as Google AdMob and Meta App Events, may automatically collect information including device identifiers, Advertising ID, approximate location derived from IP address, and app interaction data (such as app installs, feature usage, and purchase events) for advertising measurement and analytics purposes.

File Storage and Processing

All vault operations are performed locally on the user's device.

  • Photos remain stored on your device
  • Videos remain stored on your device
  • Audio files remain stored on your device
  • Files are processed locally without being uploaded to our servers
  • Files are never sold or shared with third parties
  • Selected files may be moved into secure vault storage when requested by the user
  • Original files may be removed from their previous location after a successful transfer

When a user chooses to hide a file, the application may copy the file into secure vault storage and remove the original file from its previous location after the transfer has been completed successfully.

Advertisements and Advertising Analytics

Calculator Vault uses Google AdMob to display advertisements within the application, and uses the Meta App Events SDK to measure the performance of advertising campaigns run through Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Ads.

AdMob may collect and process certain information, including device identifiers, Advertising ID, IP address, and usage data, to provide and measure advertising services.

The Meta App Events SDK may collect and process information such as device identifiers, Advertising ID, IP address, app installs, in-app events (such as onboarding completion and feature usage), and purchase events. This information is shared with Meta Platforms, Inc. to measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns and to help deliver relevant ads. Meta's use of this information is governed by Meta's Privacy Policy.

Where required by applicable laws (including in the EEA and UK), we use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request user consent before serving personalized advertisements or enabling advertising measurement through AdMob and Meta App Events. Users may manage their consent preferences through the consent dialog when available. If consent is not provided, advertising tracking and event collection through the Meta App Events SDK is disabled for that user.

For more information about how Google uses data, please visit:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps

Some Google and Meta services integrated into the application may access the Android Advertising ID for analytics, fraud prevention, diagnostics, or service functionality.

Internet Usage

  • No personal photos, videos, audio files, or vault content are uploaded to our servers
  • Internet access may be used for analytics, advertising measurement, diagnostics, application services, and purchase verification
  • Communications with third-party services are encrypted where supported

In-App Purchases

Calculator Vault may offer optional premium features through Google Play Billing.

  • All purchase transactions are processed securely by Google Play
  • We do not collect or store credit card or payment information
  • We may receive information about successful purchases and entitlement status, which may also be shared with advertising partners (such as Meta) for advertising measurement purposes

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user data and maintain application security.

  • Hidden files remain stored locally on the device
  • Files are not uploaded to developer servers
  • Access to vault content is restricted by the application's security mechanisms

Data Retention

Since files remain stored locally on your device, you control how long they remain within the application. Removing files from the vault or uninstalling the application may permanently delete vault-related data.

Children's Privacy

Calculator Vault is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page by updating the Last Updated date.

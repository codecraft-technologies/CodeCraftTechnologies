Who We Are

CodeCraft Technologies is a sole proprietorship established with a focus on software publishing and mobile application development. Based in Rahim Yar Khan, Pakistan, we are dedicated to creating innovative mobile solutions for users worldwide.

As a growing software development entity, we specialize in Android application development, utilizing modern technologies such as Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, and following Material Design guidelines to deliver high-quality user experiences.

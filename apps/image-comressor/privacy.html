App Privacy Policy

Image Toolkit

Privacy Policy for our image processing Android application.

Last Updated: August 19, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Image Toolkit ("the App"), developed and published by CodeCraft Technologies, handles information when you use the App.

Image Toolkit is designed to provide image processing tools while keeping your personal photos and images on your device. The App does not upload your images to our servers or store your images on any remote server.

Features

Image Toolkit provides various image management and processing features, including:

  • Image Compression
  • Image Resizing
  • Batch Image Processing
  • Image Format Conversion
  • Image to PDF conversion
  • Duplicate Image Removal
  • Swipe to Delete

Information We Collect

Image Toolkit does not require users to create an account, register, log in, or provide personal information directly to us.

However, the App uses certain third-party services that may collect or process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

These services include:

  • Google Firebase Analytics
  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play Billing
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) / Consent services

Firebase Analytics

Image Toolkit uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how users interact with the App and to improve its functionality, performance, and user experience.

Analytics information may include app usage information, interactions with app features, device-related information, and other technical or usage information generated through the Firebase Analytics service.

We may use analytics events related to the use of features such as image compression, resizing, conversion, PDF creation, duplicate removal, and other app interactions.

Firebase Analytics is provided by Google and may process information in accordance with Google's applicable privacy policies and terms.

Advertisements

Image Toolkit uses Google AdMob to display advertisements within the App.

Google AdMob may collect and process certain information, such as advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location information, and information related to advertising interactions, in accordance with Google's applicable policies and privacy practices.

The availability and personalization of advertisements may depend on the user's region, consent choices, device settings, and applicable laws and regulations.

Consent Management and EEA / UK Users

Image Toolkit uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) and related consent mechanisms to manage user consent for advertising and other purposes where required.

Users located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom (UK), and other applicable regions may be shown a consent request or privacy choices before certain advertising technologies are used.

Where applicable, users can make or change their consent choices through the available consent and privacy controls provided by the App.

In-App Purchases and Google Play Billing

Image Toolkit uses Google Play Billing to process In-App Purchases and/or other purchases available within the App.

Payment processing is handled by Google Play. We do not receive or store your complete payment card number, bank account information, or other full payment credentials.

Information related to purchases may be processed by Google Play in accordance with Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

Image and Media Access

Image Toolkit needs access to photos or media selected by the user in order to provide its image processing features.

Depending on the Android version, the App may use different Android media permissions to access images.

  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES: Used on supported Android versions such as Android 13 to access images selected or made available to the App.
  • READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED: Used on supported Android 14 and newer devices to support access to media specifically selected by the user.
  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: Used on supported older Android versions, up to the applicable SDK level, to access images stored on the device.
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: Used on supported older Android versions where required to save or modify processed files on the device.

Local Image Processing

Image Toolkit processes images directly on your device. Images selected for compression, resizing, conversion, PDF creation, duplicate detection, or deletion are not uploaded to our servers.

  • Images are processed locally on the device.
  • We do not upload your images to our servers.
  • We do not store your images on our servers.
  • We do not sell your photos or images.
  • We do not share your images with third parties.

The App only accesses media required to provide the feature requested by the user and according to the permissions granted through Android.

Internet and Network Access

Image Toolkit requires Internet access for certain third-party services and online functionality, including advertisements, analytics, consent management, and Google Play services.

The App may use the following permissions:

  • INTERNET: Required for communication with applicable online services such as advertising, analytics, consent management, and Google Play services.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used to determine the current network connectivity status.

Notifications

Image Toolkit may request the POST_NOTIFICATIONS permission on supported Android versions when notifications are used.

Notification permission is requested according to Android's permission system. Users can allow or deny notification access through their device settings.

Third-Party Services

The App integrates third-party services provided by Google. These services may process information independently of Image Toolkit according to their respective privacy policies, terms, and applicable laws.

  • Google Firebase Analytics
  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play Billing
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)
  • Google Play Services where applicable

We recommend reviewing Google's privacy documentation for additional information about how these services handle data.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information or your photos. Image Toolkit does not upload your photos or images to our servers for storage or processing.

However, information may be processed by integrated third-party services such as Google Firebase Analytics, Google AdMob, Google Play Billing, and Google consent services as necessary to provide their respective functionality.

Data Retention

Image Toolkit does not maintain a remote database containing your personal information or uploaded photos.

Data generated or stored locally by the App remains subject to your device's storage and Android's file management system. You can remove locally stored files using the App or your device's file management tools, where applicable.

Third-party services may retain information according to their own policies and retention practices.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information handled by the App. Image processing is performed locally on your device, which means your photos are not transmitted to our servers for processing.

However, no electronic system or method of transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's Privacy

Image Toolkit does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

The App does not require users to create an account or submit personal information to use its core image processing features.

Third-party services integrated into the App may have their own age-related policies and requirements.

Your Choices

You can control certain permissions through your Android device settings, including access to photos, media, and notifications.

Where applicable, advertising and consent choices can also be managed through the consent controls provided by the App or through applicable device and Google settings.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App, third-party services, legal requirements, or our privacy practices.

Any changes will be reflected on this page by updating the "Last Updated" date.

App Privacy Policy

Image Compressor – Resize & Convert

Privacy Policy for our image processing Android application.

Last Updated: February 6, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Image Compressor – Resize & Convert handles information when you use the app. The app is designed with a strong focus on user privacy and simplicity.

Information Collection

Image Compressor – Resize & Convert does not collect, store, or share any personal or non-personal information.

  • No personal data is collected
  • No usage data is tracked
  • No analytics or tracking tools are used
  • No login or account is required

Advertisements

Image Compressor – Resize & Convert does not display any advertisements.

  • No ad networks are integrated
  • No advertising identifiers are used
  • No data is shared for advertising purposes

Image Processing

All image compression, resizing, and format conversion happens locally on your device.

  • Images are never uploaded to any server
  • Images are not shared with any third party
  • The app only accesses images you select using Android's system file picker

Internet Usage

The app works completely offline.

  • No internet connection is required
  • No background network activity

Third-Party Services

Image Compressor – Resize & Convert does not use any third-party services.

Data Security

Since no data is collected or transmitted, your images and personal information remain fully private and secure on your device.

Children's Privacy

The app does not collect any data and is safe to use for users of all ages.

Changes to This Policy

Any changes to this Privacy Policy will be reflected on this page by updating the "Last Updated" date.

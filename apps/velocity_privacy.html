App Privacy Policy

Velocity VPN

Privacy Policy for our high-speed, secure, and private Virtual Private Network (VPN) Android application.

Last Updated: September 29, 2026

Introduction

Velocity VPN ("the App") is designed to provide secure, encrypted, and private internet access on Android devices. Protecting user privacy and digital freedom is our highest priority.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the application accesses, how it is used, and how it is protected under global standards including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Strict No-Logs Policy

Velocity VPN operates under a strict, verifiable Zero-Logs policy. We do not track, collect, store, or log any of the following:

  • Browsing history, visited websites, or search queries
  • Traffic payload, downloaded files, or uploaded data
  • DNS queries or destination IP addresses
  • Your real originating IP address when connected to our VPN servers
  • Timestamps linking user activity to specific servers

We never sell, rent, or monetize your network traffic or personal browsing data under any circumstances.

Android VpnService & Tunneling Operations

Velocity VPN uses the official Android VpnService API to establish encrypted Virtual Private Network tunnels using the industry-standard OpenVPN protocol.

  • The VpnService tunnel is used exclusively to route your device's traffic through secure, encrypted VPN servers
  • All network traffic is encrypted using authenticated cryptographic protocols (AES-128-GCM and AES-256-GCM)
  • The VPN tunnel only activates when explicitly initiated by the user tapping "Connect"
  • The user can disconnect the VPN tunnel at any time with a single tap

Information We Do NOT Collect

Velocity VPN does not require account registration or passwords to use the core VPN service. We do not collect personally identifiable information such as:

  • Name
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number
  • Contacts or Address Book
  • Messages or SMS
  • Photos, Videos, or Device Files
  • Personal Documents

Third-Party Services

The application uses the following trusted third-party services to provide advertising, subscription billing, and cloud configuration sync:

  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play Billing
  • Google Firebase (Firestore & Anonymous Authentication)
  • Google Play Services

Each of these services may independently collect and process information as described in the sections below. Please review each provider's own privacy policy for further detail on how they process data.

Google AdMob (Advertisements)

Velocity VPN uses Google AdMob to display advertisements within the free tier of the application, including:

  • Banner Ads: Displayed cleanly at the bottom of the Home, Server Selection, and Settings screens
  • Interstitial Ads: Full-screen ads shown upon user-initiated Connect and Disconnect actions, subject to policy-compliant frequency cooldowns
  • App Open Ads: Brief ads displayed when the application is launched or resumed from the background

AdMob may collect and process certain information, including device identifiers, the Android Advertising ID (GAID), IP address, and coarse usage data, to provide and measure advertising services and prevent fraud.

For more information about how Google uses data, please visit:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps

Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) & GDPR / EEA Consent

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and equivalent local laws apply to how your data is processed.

To support compliance with these laws, Velocity VPN uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to determine whether a user is located in a region requiring consent, and to present a consent form before any consent-requiring data collection or ad personalization takes place.

  • Advertising personalization through Google AdMob is only enabled after consent is obtained through the UMP consent form, where required.
  • If consent is not granted, or the UMP flow determines ads cannot be requested with personalization, the app continues to function normally, serving only non-personalized, contextual advertisements.
  • Users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland have rights under the GDPR, including the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of their personal data, and the right to withdraw consent at any time.
  • Because Velocity VPN adheres to a strict No-Logs policy and does not store personal records on our servers, there is no personal browsing activity or identity data stored to disclose or delete.

In-App Purchases & VIP Subscriptions

Velocity VPN may offer optional premium / VIP features through Google Play Billing, such as unlocking high-speed PRO server locations and removing all advertisements.

  • All purchase transactions are processed securely by Google Play
  • We do not collect, process, or store credit card or payment information
  • We only receive a digital confirmation from Google Play regarding your entitlement status to unlock PRO features

Firebase & Cloud Configuration

The app uses Google Firebase to securely sync available server locations:

  • Firebase Anonymous Authentication: Issues a temporary, pseudonymous token so the app can verify genuine client access without collecting your personal identity
  • Cloud Firestore: Stores real-time VPN node connection endpoints (IP, port, protocol) to keep server lists up to date

Data Security

We take comprehensive technical and organizational measures to protect user data and maintain application security:

  • All network tunnels are encrypted with authenticated OpenVPN AES-GCM standards
  • No browsing activity or user payload is ever routed to developer servers
  • Firestore database access is governed by cloud security rules strictly locked to authorized administrator accounts

Data Retention

Because Velocity VPN does not log browsing activity, visited URLs, or user traffic, there is zero user activity data stored or retained. Ephemeral connection state exists only in temporary device memory (RAM) while the VPN tunnel is active and is completely discarded upon disconnection.

Children's Privacy

Velocity VPN is not intended for children under the age of 13 (or under 16 for residents of the EEA/UK). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page by updating the Last Updated date.

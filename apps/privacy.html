Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 30, 2026

Welcome to Recover It.

Your privacy is our top priority. This app operates entirely on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal data to any external servers.

1. How Recover It Works

Recover It allows users to view previously received notifications. The app uses Notification Access permission to read notifications shown on your device.

  • Only apps selected by the user are processed
  • The app does NOT monitor all apps automatically

2. What Data is Accessed

When enabled, the app may access:

  • Notification text
  • Sender name (as shown)
  • Timestamp

This data comes only from notifications already visible on your device.

3. Local Storage (No Data Leaves Your Device)

  • All data is stored in a local database
  • Stored only on your device
  • Never uploaded or shared

4. What We Do NOT Do

  • No direct access to private messages
  • No bypassing encryption
  • No cloud sync
  • No data selling or sharing

5. User Control

  • You choose which apps to monitor
  • You can disable access anytime
  • You can delete data anytime
  • Uninstall removes all data

6. Permissions Used

Notification Access Permission

Used only to read notifications from selected apps.

Storage Permission (if applicable)

Used only to store app-related content such as notifications, stickers, and cache. All data remains on your device.

Sticker Storage & WhatsApp Integration

  • Stickers are stored locally on your device
  • No sticker data is uploaded
  • Uses official WhatsApp sticker integration methods
  • Stickers are validated to meet platform requirements

7. Third-Party Services

This app does NOT use analytics tools or tracking services.

Future versions may include ads, and third-party policies will apply if enabled.

8. Data Security

Since all data is stored locally, no personal data is transmitted externally. You are responsible for securing your device.

9. Disclaimer

Recover It is NOT affiliated with or endorsed by WhatsApp Inc.

WhatsApp is a registered trademark of WhatsApp Inc.

This app only reads notifications and does not access encrypted messages.

10. Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for children under 13.

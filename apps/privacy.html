Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 3, 2026

Welcome to Recover It.

We respect your privacy. This app primarily works on your device, however certain features such as analytics, advertising, and subscriptions involve limited data processing by trusted third-party services.

1. How Recover It Works

Recover It allows users to view previously received notifications using Notification Access permission.

  • Only user-selected apps are monitored
  • The app does NOT monitor all apps automatically

2. What Data is Accessed

  • Notification text
  • Sender name
  • Timestamp

This data is taken only from notifications already visible on your device.

3. Local Storage

  • Notification data is stored locally on your device
  • No personal messages are uploaded by the app

4. Analytics (Google Firebase Analytics)

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand app usage and improve performance.

  • App usage statistics
  • Device information (such as device model, OS version)
  • Crash and performance data

This data does NOT include your personal messages or notification content.

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), analytics processing complies with GDPR requirements.

5. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

The app may offer premium features via subscriptions or one-time purchases.

  • Payments are processed securely via Google Play Billing
  • We do NOT collect or store your payment details
  • Google may process purchase-related data under its own policies

6. Advertising (Google AdMob)

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements.

  • AdMob may collect device information, IP address, and advertising ID
  • Ads may be personalized based on your interests
  • Google may use cookies or similar technologies

Users in the European Economic Area (EEA) may be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

You can manage ad personalization through your device settings.

7. Permissions Used

Notification Access

Used only to read notifications from selected apps.

Storage (if applicable)

Used to store app data such as notifications, stickers, and cache locally on your device.

WhatsApp Sticker Integration

  • Uses official WhatsApp sticker integration methods
  • Stickers are stored locally
  • No sticker data is uploaded

8. Third-Party Services

  • Google Firebase Analytics
  • Google Play Billing
  • Google AdMob

These services may collect and process data according to their own privacy policies.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data. Most data remains stored locally on your device.

10. GDPR & EU User Rights

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have rights under GDPR.

  • Access your data
  • Request correction or deletion
  • Restrict or object to processing
  • Withdraw consent

For EEA users, consent is requested where required, especially for personalized advertising.

Some data may be processed outside your country by trusted providers with appropriate safeguards.

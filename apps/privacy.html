Last Updated: April 26, 2026

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 26, 2026

Welcome to Recover It.

Your privacy is our top priority. This app is designed to operate entirely on your device, and we do not collect or transmit your personal data to any external servers.

1. How Recover It Works

Recover It provides a feature that allows users to view previously received notifications.

The app works by using the Notification Access permission, which enables it to read notifications that appear on your device.

👉 Important:

  • The app processes notifications only from apps that you manually select inside the app
  • The app does NOT monitor all apps automatically

2. What Data is Accessed

When you enable notification access and select specific apps:

The app may access:

  • Notification text
  • Sender name (as shown in notification)
  • Timestamp of the notification

⚠️ This data is obtained only from notifications already visible on your device

3. Local Storage (No Data Leaves Your Device)

All accessed notification data is:

  • Stored in a local database (Room database)
  • Saved only on your device
  • Never uploaded to any server

👉 We do NOT store, process, or transmit your messages outside your device.

4. What We Do NOT Do

Recover It does NOT:

  • Access private messages directly from any app
  • Bypass encryption or security of messaging apps
  • Upload or sync your data to cloud/server
  • Share your data with third parties
  • Sell or monetize your personal messages

5. User Control

You remain in full control at all times:

  • You choose which apps are included
  • You can disable notification access anytime
  • You can delete all stored data from within the app
  • Uninstalling the app removes all stored data

6. Permissions Used

Notification Access Permission

Used only to read notifications from user-selected apps to provide in-app functionality.

Storage Permission (if applicable)

Used only for saving app-related content such as stickers or cached data, all stored locally on your device.

Sticker Storage

Stickers used in the app are stored locally on your device in a private app directory. No sticker data is transmitted to external servers.

7. Analytics (Non-Personal Data)

We may use tools such as Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage data, including:

  • App performance
  • Feature usage
  • Crash reports

This data does NOT include message content or personal conversations.

8. Third-Party Services

We may use:

  • Google Firebase Analytics
  • AdMob (for advertisements, if enabled)

These services may collect anonymized information as per their own privacy policies.

9. Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device, no personal message data is transmitted externally.

You are responsible for securing access to your device.

10. Disclaimer

Recover It is an independent application and is not affiliated with WhatsApp, Telegram, or any other messaging platform.

The app works solely by reading notifications generated by these apps.

11. Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for children under the age of 13.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be reflected within the app.

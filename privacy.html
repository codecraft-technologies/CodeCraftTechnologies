Privacy Policy

How we handle your information and protect your privacy.

Last Updated: February 6, 2026

Introduction

CodeCraft Technologies ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile applications and services.

Current Development Status

Our mobile applications are currently under development. At this stage:

  • No user data is being collected
  • No personal information is stored or processed
  • No third-party analytics or tracking services are integrated
  • No advertisements are displayed

This policy will be updated as our applications progress through development and additional features are implemented.

Information We May Collect in the Future

As our applications are developed and released, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, and unique device identifiers
  • Usage Data: How you interact with our applications, features used, and performance data
  • Crash Reports: Information about application crashes to help us improve stability

We will update this Privacy Policy before implementing any data collection and will clearly inform users of any changes.

Data Security

We are committed to ensuring that your information is secure. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction of any data we may collect in the future.

Third-Party Services

Our applications may use third-party services in the future, such as:

  • Google Play Services
  • Firebase Analytics
  • Crash reporting services

When implemented, these services will have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them.

Children's Privacy

Our applications are not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it immediately.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

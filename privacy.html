App Privacy Policy

Image Compressor – Resize & Convert

Privacy Policy for our image processing Android application.

Last Updated: May 16, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Image Compressor – Resize & Convert ("the App") handles information when you use our Android application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding how data is processed.

Information Collection

We do not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or contacts directly through the app.

  • No account or registration is required
  • No personal photos are uploaded to our servers
  • Only limited anonymous usage data may be collected

Analytics

The app uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand app usage, monitor performance, and improve user experience.

  • Anonymous device and usage information may be collected
  • No personally identifiable information is intentionally collected
  • Analytics data is used only to improve app functionality and stability

Advertisements

This app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and process certain information to provide personalized and non-personalized ads.

  • Google AdMob may use Advertising ID for advertising purposes
  • Third-party advertising partners may collect anonymous device information
  • Ads may be personalized based on user interests and interactions
  • Users in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom (UK), and Switzerland may see a consent request in compliance with GDPR

Advertising ID

The app may use your device’s Advertising ID to show relevant ads, measure ad effectiveness, and improve advertising experience.

  • Advertising ID is a resettable identifier provided by Android
  • You can reset or disable personalized advertising in device settings
  • No personally identifiable information is linked to the Advertising ID

GDPR & User Consent

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom (UK), or Switzerland, we comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

  • Users may be asked to provide consent for personalized ads
  • Users can choose personalized or non-personalized advertisements
  • Consent preferences may be changed anytime through device settings
  • We may use Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to collect and manage consent

Image Processing

All image compression, resizing, and conversion operations are performed locally on your device.

  • Images are never uploaded to our servers
  • Images are not shared with third parties
  • The app only processes images selected by the user
  • Processed images are stored locally on your device

Storage Permission

The app may request storage-related permissions to save processed images.

  • Android 9 and below may require storage permission
  • Android 10 and above use scoped storage where applicable
  • Permissions are only used for app functionality

Internet Usage

The app may use internet access for analytics, advertising, and app-related services.

  • No personal images or files are uploaded without user action
  • Internet access may be used to load advertisements from AdMob
  • Anonymous analytics and advertising data may be transmitted securely

Third-Party Services

The app may use the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase Analytics
  • Google AdMob
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)

Data Security

We value your privacy and take reasonable measures to protect your information. Your images remain stored locally on your device.

Children's Privacy

The app is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page by updating the "Last Updated" date.
